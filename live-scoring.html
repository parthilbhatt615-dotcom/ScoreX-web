Normal scoring

Record one delivery at a time. Runs update the team total and relevant player/bowler information while legal deliveries advance the over state.

Extras

Wide, No Ball, Bye and Leg Bye are treated according to their scoring meaning and legal-ball behavior.

Wickets & Run Out

A wicket updates dismissal state. Run Out can preserve completed runs, so the over can show 1 + W, 2 + W or 4 + W rather than discarding the runs.

No Ball + 4

The no-ball extra and batsman runs remain separate components of the same delivery event.
