What ScoreX Solves

Built around the real needs of a scorer.

Manual cricket scoring requires continuous calculations and synchronized tracking. ScoreX records delivery events so related match information stays coordinated.

01

Complete Match Flow

Match setup, opening players, live scoring, innings transitions, result calculation and complete match review.

⚡
02

Special Deliveries

Normal runs, Wide, No Ball, Bye, Leg Bye, wickets and Run Out with completed runs retained.

📊
03

Match Analysis

Batting, bowling, partnership and over information with runs-per-over, run-rate and innings comparison graphs.

↩
04

Safe Corrections

Undo the most recent scoring action instead of manually compensating for a scoring mistake.

ScoreX • Cricket Scorer

Everything you need to score a match.

ScoreX is a local-first Android cricket scoring application designed to make manual scoring faster, clearer and more reliable.

🏏 Live Scoring

Record runs, wickets, extras and delivery events while ScoreX keeps the match state synchronized.

📊 Automatic Statistics

Track batting, bowling, overs, partnerships, extras and run-rate information during the match.

📄 Scorecards & PDF

Review the completed scorecard and generate a shareable PDF report.

📈 Graphs

Compare innings and visualize runs-per-over and scoring progression.

🕘 Match History

Keep completed matches locally and reopen them for review.

↩ Undo

Correct the most recent scoring action without manually reversing every statistic.

How ScoreX Works

From match setup to final scorecard.

Create the match, configure the rules, enter opening players, score every delivery, complete the innings and review the result, statistics, graphs and PDF scorecard.
